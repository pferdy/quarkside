The soon to be late lamented Audit Commission did some excellent work in examining the performance of Councils.

<em><strong>"In 2008, only 5 per cent of councils were assessed as having excellent data quality.  And the position has not improved. In 2009, the majority of councils were found to be meeting only the basic requirements of data quality."</strong></em>

Danny Budzak, on Kingston University Public Sector IMKS Forum, said that he is very wary of this figure. Working with Newcastle University on a project around Information Governance across all the local authorities and fire and rescue services in the north east looked at was data quality.  They found data is not just duplicated - it's often multi-replicated.  Systems are over-procured and under-utilised with too many software licences.

Without good data quality, data sharing is too dangerous.  Data cleansing is an essential first step - too expensive in the current climate.
