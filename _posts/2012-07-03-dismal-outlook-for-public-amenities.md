The Local Government Association (LGA) has published a most depressing scenario of what the public can expect from Local Authorities (LAs).  It is all based on the <a href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/funding.pdf" target="_blank">projected funding for 2019 - 2020</a>.

They have looked at <strong><em>"unavoidable statutory obligations"</em></strong> as the foundation and deemed  it to cover social care and environment(waste) only.  Look at the rate that non-statutory services will have their funding reduced.

So that means a reduction of two thirds for treasured things like leisure.  Here's a percentage view. (Note that schools are excluded).
