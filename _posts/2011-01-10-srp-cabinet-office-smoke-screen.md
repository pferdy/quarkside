It's good to see from the latest update that the Cabinet Office has taken responsibility for structural reform of ICT <a href="http://bit.ly/dPEPCv" target="_blank">(Sections 1.9 to 1.13)</a>.  It was intriguing to see something which is entirely sensible:

<em><strong>1.9.i. Increase the Chief Information Officer’s power to integrate ICT across government. (Completed)</strong></em>

But, it begs a few of questions:
<ul>
	<li>When was it completed?</li>
	<li>What was the instrument for increasing that power in central and local government?</li>
	<li>What tools have been provided to enable this integration?</li>
	<li>Who is the CIO?</li>
</ul>
Research of earlier transparency web site records show that:
<ul>
	<li>The July baseline only included 5 items in Section 1 (Civil Service Reform).</li>
	<li>The <a href="http://bit.ly/g1L5kA" target="_blank">October 2010  Progress reports</a> can only be generated for months from November 2010 onwards.</li>
</ul>
There have obviously been a number of changes to the Cabinet Office SRP, but where are the documents?

There are lots more completed actions - but the results do not seem to have been published.  This more like a smoke screen than a telescope for transparency.
