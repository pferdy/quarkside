Liam Maxwell has published "<a href="http://bit.ly/aqr64l">Better for Less</a>"  Most of the conclusions are well argued and they should be taken forward to influence public sector ICT policy.

What I do like are:
	<li>Goal: Lower cost technology delivery.</li>
	<li>Guiding principles: Open, local, privacy, outcomes focussed, government controlled</li>
	<li>Four workstreams:  Audit, Identity, skills, Open Market</li>
If I were a politician then I could understand the messages and provide leadership to make sure it happens.

If I were a CIO in central or local government, then I could adapt the messages to suit local conditions and service priorities
