A recent Cabinet Office presentation of the <a href="http://www.cabinetoffice.gov.uk/news/second-g-cloud-supplier-framework-goes-live" target="_blank">GCloud</a> to SOCITM London had some good news, and some things to do better.
<p style="padding-left:30px;"><strong>Good</strong>:  There are lots more suppliers and the offerings are much simpler, and less costly to purchase.  They are preparing the terms and conditions for the next round and consulting on changes that make sense.  Suppliers in the audience pressed there need for longer contracts; even two years is not enough for the more complex requirements of information sharing in local government.</p>
<p style="padding-left:30px;"><strong>Must do better</strong>:  The presentation did not mention standards.  The messages from <a href="http://wp.me/p14MGf-s2" target="_blank">LeGSB</a> are not getting through. Virtually the whole audience thought there was room for suppliers to say which interoperability standards they would use. It is the only way to develop multi-agency services from bottom-up.</p>
Will we see the resurgence of the e-GIF and LGIP (Local Government Integration Practice)?

Will we advance as far as <a href="http://wp.me/p14MGf-9b" target="_blank">India</a>?

Will <a href="http://wp.me/p14MGf-qN" target="_blank">Liam Maxwell's targets for Open Standards</a> be met?
