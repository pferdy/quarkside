Why will chains of block eventually fade into the shadows of tech?

* Energy and cost economics: Replicated, trustless verification is slower and more expensive than a centralised database. For almost any business problem, a normal database with an audit log does the same job cheaper. Technology that costs more for worse performance survives on ideology, not utility.

* Trust: Most economic activity already relies on contracts, courts, banks and reputation. Where decentralisation does matter, the public reputation is poor: FTX, hacks and scams. Even the mighty Microsoft struggled through Friday 13th. Even trusted intermediaries have single points of failure.

* Speculation: Strip away trading and genuine adoption is thin. Regulators refuse to let unbacked tokens near mainstream finance. CBDCs are banned in the USA. Infrastructure funded by rising token prices is a bubble with an API.

Likely outcome: Distributed ledgers persist as a niche accounting technique. "Blockchain" joins "semantic web" as a technology nobody remembers.
