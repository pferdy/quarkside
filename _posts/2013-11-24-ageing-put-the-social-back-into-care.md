At last, some recognition from people who understand the need to put the Social back into Care.  The "SmokingChimney" prototype won a £2000 prize for the Kent Connects challenge "<a href="http://www.simpl.co/challenges/31-transformed-by-you/ideas" target="_blank">How can we make it easier for people to help each other?</a>"

It was not led by Technology - but a real life story from one of the attendees who joined the team.  He was so impressed that he wants the first version off the production line.  He created the strapline and enthusiastically gave the winning presentation.  It is an example of how designers should listen to people who want something very simple - the refinements can wait.

Have a look at the <a title="Kemuri" href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/prototype1a.ppt" target="_blank">SmokingChimney</a> slide pack to see the simplicity of the prototype.
