The Care Bill will need massive ICT change within Local Authorities (LAs) by 2016.  The Department of Health (DH) have issued, for discussion, a guidance note on social care information and technology (<a href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/guidance.pdf">DH Care Bill Guidance</a>).  Priority actions are:

</div>
</div>
</div>
<ol>
	<li><strong>Roadmap:</strong> Plan with suppliers to satisfy a new charging regime by 2015.  Back office systems will need updating.  LAs will also have to provide information and advice to citizens - especially signposting citizens to care services.</li>
	<li><strong>Self-Funders:</strong>  Consider on-line assessment for self‐funders within wider changes in information and systems.</li>
	<li><strong>Better Care Fund:</strong>  Use the NHS Number as the primary identifier to comply with Integrated Digital Care Records Guidance.  There is capital fund open to NHS providers applying for resources to support the development of Integrated Digital Care Records, including with adult social care.  The second round will open shortly and LAs are strongly encouraged to work with NHS providers to make a compelling proposal.</li>
	<li><strong>Open APIs: </strong>  Think about embracing open APIs when procuring, renewing or refreshing its IT systems.</li>
	<li><strong>Technology Enablement:</strong>  Work with DH and national partners, to develop technology that drives improved outcomes, citizen experience and efficiency.</li>
</ol>
LAs should take this opportunity to consider joining forces to support a social enterprise that provides a national infrastructure with Open APIs into citizen controlled Personal Data Stores, keyed on the NHS number.  Co-production is the best opportunity in decades to provide interoperability between health and social care records, including self-funders recording expenditure against their Personal Care Budgets.  A social enterprise, such as <a title="Funding a Social Enterprise" href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/2014/01/24/funding-a-social-enterprise/" target="_blank">Kemuri</a>, could provide such an infrastructure, with suitable funding.
