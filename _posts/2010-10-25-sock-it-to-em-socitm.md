Since governance matters, all local authority Chief Execs and CIOs should read this hard punching <a href="http://bit.ly/dAzVwP">briefing</a>.  SOCITM concludes that the cost problem of cost can be solved by
<ul>
	<li>collaboration, reformation and innovation;</li>
	<li>ownership and control of information shifting towards individuals not organisations ...</li>
	<li>shared services provisioned over the cloud</li>
</ul>
They even extend Liam Maxwell's strap line "<a href="http://wp.me/p14MGf-18">better for less</a>" as  "achieve more, better, with less".

More important are the six principles to take us forward in a reducing budget environment
<ol>
	<li>Portfolio and programme management</li>
	<li>Information management – architecture, standards, assurance and security.</li>
	<li>Public Services ICT Infrastructure – Public Sector Network (PSN) and cloud provisioning, including shared data centres and applications.</li>
	<li>Shared services and supplier management.</li>
	<li>Access devices.</li>
	<li>Sustainability and green ICT.</li>
</ol>
I like it better than the <a href="http://wp.me/p14MGf-1n">incomplete ICT life cycle</a> in "Better for Less",  but I must declare an interest as a member of the SOCITM Futures group.  Innovation cannot take place without Chief Executives <a href="http://wp.me/p14MGf-20">ring fencing of funds</a> in a managed <a href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/2010/09/27/better-for-less-could-do-better/trackback/">portfolio of projects</a> and continuing services.
