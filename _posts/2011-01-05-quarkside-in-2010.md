<h2>Crunchy numbers</h2>

A Boeing 747-400 passenger jet can hold 416 passengers.  This blog was viewed about <strong>1,700</strong> times in 2010.  That's about 4 full 747s.

In 2010, there were <strong>76</strong> new posts, not bad for the first year! There were <strong>11</strong> pictures uploaded, taking up a total of 5mb. That's about a picture per month.

The busiest day of the year was November 11th with <strong>192</strong> views. The most popular post that day was <a style="color:#08c;" href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/2010/11/10/sif-strikes-back/">SIF Strikes back</a>.
<h2>Where did they come from?</h2>
The top referring sites in 2010 were <strong>linkedin.com</strong>, <strong>twitter.com</strong>, <strong>edugeek.net</strong>, <strong>en.wordpress.com</strong>, and <strong>hootsuite.com</strong>.

Some visitors came searching, mostly for <strong>quarkside</strong>, <strong>dr len anderson ict</strong>, <strong>quarkside blog</strong>, <strong>sif dfe</strong>, and <strong>quarkside.co.uk  len anderson</strong>.

As a result of this, both quarkside.co.uk and quarkside.com domains have been secured.  They will just redirect to the Wordpress site as we have the technology!
