<div>Ageing impacts everybody in civil society.  We all grow older; we observe the ageing process with mild disinterest until it affects us personally. Who are the key stakeholders in sustaining a reasonable quality of life for our ever increasing ageing population?</div>
<ul>
	<li>
<div>Older people living independently</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Relatives, who may live many miles away</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Carers providing voluntary or commercial services</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Local Authority Social Services officers</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Voluntary Sector advisors</div></li>
</ul>
At the <a href="http://www.meetup.com/HealthTechnologyForum-London/events/146020572/" target="_blank">Health Technology Forum</a> (November 20th, 2013), <a href="http://storify.com/clarkmike" target="_blank">Mike Clark</a> informed us that there are 1.5 million people employed in the Social Care industry.  In addition, there are about 5 million more providing unpaid care to relatives, friends or via voluntary work.  Before may years have passed, more then 9 million of the UK population will be personally involved in providing social care.   It is beginning to have a major impact on UK productivity with so much time and effort being deployed in caring. Technology enables people to do more, with less effort, improving outcomes in health and wellbeing.

Before rushing into a care technology solution, Quarkside recommends taking stock of what each stakeholder group needs, with answers to simple questions for each group:
<ul>
	<li>
<div>What are the benefits of monitoring activity in the home?</div></li>
	<li>
<div>What are the risks?</div></li>
	<li>
<div>How much activity should be monitored and stored, if any?</div></li>
	<li>
<div>How much data should be shared, and with whom?</div></li>
	<li>
<div>What are the critical privacy requirements?</div></li>
	<li>
<div>What is affordable?</div></li>
</ul>
These questions are being posed at <a href="http://www.simpl.co/challenges/31-transformed-by-you/ideas/706" target="_blank">Kent County Council</a> (November 23rd, 2013).  The results should should inform any care ecosystem design for the technology products that will largely have to be <a href="http://wp.me/14MGf" target="_blank">self-funded</a>.  The State will not provide for things that only provide peace of mind and improved levels of wellbeing of older people and their relatives.
