We may applaud the <a title="MLF supports open standards: Open Care EcoSystem?" href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/2013/07/13/mlf-supports-open-standards-open-care-ecosystem/" target="_blank">rhetoric for standards</a> enabling better joined up outcomes.  The landscape for interoperability for health and social care systems seems to have boundaries that disappear to infinity.  There's no better illustration than the "Infinity Diagram" from a <a href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/review1.pdf" target="_blank">TSB publication</a>.

[caption id="attachment_2245" align="aligncenter" width="528"]<a href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/inf1.jpg"><img class="size-full wp-image-2245" alt="Infinity Diagram" src="http://lenand.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/inf1.jpg" width="528" height="325" /></a> Infinity Diagram[/caption]

<a href="https://connect.innovateuk.org/web/assisted-living-innovation-platform-alip/article-view/-/blogs/alip-standards-meeting-25th-march-2013-review-of-standards-roadmap?p_p_auth=h6cLTaYI&amp;_33_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fconnect.innovateuk.org%2Fweb%2Fassisted-living-innovation-platform-alip%2Farticles%3Fp_p_id%3D101_INSTANCE_okNCIW6dT09i%26p_p_lifecycle%3D0%26p_p_state%3Dnormal%26p_p_mode%3Dview%26p_p_col_id%3Dcolumn-1%26p_p_col_count%3D1" target="_blank">Standards</a> are needed throughout, but most development has been focussed in the public and statutory sectors.   The crisis in state funding is forcing more care onto citizens so they must be brought into a standards framework.  This is happening via <a href="http://ifocus-dallas.com/pub/?page_id=263" target="_blank">i3i</a>, a consortium committed to designing open technical standards for the global delivery of assisted living services.
