<a href="http://www.kemuri.org.uk" target="_blank">Kemuri</a> wants more people to share this vision:
<p style="text-align:center;"><span style="color:#ff0000;"><strong>People living longer, happier and healthier </strong></span></p>
<p style="text-align:center;"><span style="color:#ff0000;"><strong>in their own homes </strong></span></p>
<p style="text-align:center;"><span style="color:#ff0000;"><strong>with non-intrusive social support </strong></span></p>
<p style="text-align:center;"><span style="color:#ff0000;"><strong>from family, friends and carers </strong></span></p>
<a href="http://www.kemuri.org.uk" target="_blank">Kemuri</a> products and services will help towards achieving it for more families of vulnerable people living alone.  <a href="http://www.kemuri.org.uk" target="_blank">Kemuri</a> has entered a competition, "Cisco Internet of Things Innovation Grand Challenge". The winner will receive a prize worth US$200,000. Winning would boost the ability to scale quickly to a service for thousands of families with older people living alone. A place in the semi-finals is guaranteed if the entry "<a href="https://iotchallenge.cisco.spigit.com/Page/ViewIdea?ideaid=634" target="_blank">Social care costs reduced with IoT sensors and predictive monitoring</a>" is "liked" by the most number of people. To make sure that people are genuinely interested, <a href="https://iotchallenge.cisco.spigit.com/Page/Home" target="_blank">Cisco</a> require people to <a href="https://iotchallenge.cisco.spigit.com/User/Register" target="_blank">register</a> on the Innovation web site.

The publicity for being top of the poll would be useful, even if it does not lead to winning the top prize.  Spreading the <a href="https://iotchallenge.cisco.spigit.com/Page/ViewIdea?ideaid=634" target="_blank">link</a> would help to gather a few more votes for a worthwhile <a href="https://lenand.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/fly140523.pdf" target="_blank">social enterprise</a> that could have an impact on every family that is concerned about the wellbeing of vulnerable people living alone.
