Google has sent out new terms and conditions for a Wallet. They refer to GPL without a definition.  All we get is:
<p style="padding-left:30px;">"<strong>GPL Website"</strong> means a website of GPL or its Subsidiaries or Affiliates.</p>
<p style="padding-left:30px;">"<strong>GPL"</strong>, "we" or "us" means GPL Payment Limited.</p>
It is possible to guess, but in a 20 odd pages of fine print, you would think they could define their most important TLA (Three Letter Acronym).

Quarkside declined the offer.
