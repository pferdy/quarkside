Friday 13 November 2026. Centralised systems failed and impacted everybody. They don't scale. One network upgrade stopped vital civilian services: Outlook, Teams, Office 365, Azure.

The same day: a nationwide ground stop on US airspace. Officially unconnected. Make of that what you will.

Reason: Centralised

* Everything in a few places
* Run by a few companies
* One failure, everyone down

That is not resilience. That is fragility with a service-level agreement.

Alternative: Decentralised

* Many nodes, many places, many owners
* No single point of failure
* Keep operating when one node goes down
* Scale infinitely
* Federate with many trusted partners

It only needs the telecoms network to be resilient and keep the Internet running.
