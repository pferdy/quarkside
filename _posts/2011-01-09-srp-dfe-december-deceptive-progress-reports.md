It's good to see the latest SRP updates and the new <a href="http://bit.ly/g7E0MV" target="_blank">web pages for each Departmen</a>t.  The new pages are referenced with a number that purportedly matches to the baseline - so much better.

BUT BUT BUT

Just looking at <a href="http://bit.ly/g9gV27" target="_blank">DfE, the baseline</a> in July just published 3 priority areas.  The <a href="http://bit.ly/eQckjF" target="_blank">December DfE update</a> has  6 Priority areas.  When were the extras added?  Where is the change control that justifies the increase in scope?  Not only that, the contents have changed, for example, from:
<p style="padding-left:30px;"><strong>1.1  Allow all schools to apply for Academy status </strong></p>
<p style="padding-left:30px;"><strong>1.2  Enable the takeover by an experienced education provider of underperforming schools</strong></p>
<p style="padding-left:30px;"><strong>1.3   Make it easier for new providers to open new Schools</strong></p>
To:
<p style="padding-left:30px;"><strong> 1.1 Increase the number of Academies </strong></p>
<p style="padding-left:30px;"><strong>1.2 Introduce new Free Schools </strong></p>
<p style="padding-left:30px;"><strong>1.3 Introduce new University Technical Colleges </strong></p>
It may be that the new priorities are better - <strong>but</strong> the programme is transparently out of control by any normal standards of programme management.  It's no better, or even worse, than the November report where the<a href="http://wp.me/p14MGf-cQ" target="_blank"> DfE obscured delays</a>.  Uncontrolled change is a key indicator of risk in a programme.

Is <a title="Kristina’s kidding PM" href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/2010/12/13/kristinas-kidding-pm/" target="_blank">Kristina</a> aware of the importance of what is happening? Are the other departments being equally devious?  Should the PM be told?
