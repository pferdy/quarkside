DCLG don't know the full range <a href="http://bit.ly/gTIKJF" target="_blank">statutory duties</a> placed on local authorities.  Most arise from primary legislation, and DCLG are aware they don't have a complete list - and they already have 1294 of them.  Hence, they are asking local government to comment on their duties and to challenge those which are burdensome or no longer needed.  A consultation is asking:
<ul>
	<li><em>What duties are vital to keep?</em></li>
	<li><em>What duties should be repealed?</em></li>
	<li><em>What burdens have been created through particular duties, and associated regulations and guidance?</em></li>
	<li><em>What duties have not been included on the attached list and should also be considered in the review</em>?</li>
</ul>
Some regulations go back to early Victorian times, such as the London Hackney Carriages Act 1843 Section 19	- "<em>Transport for London To replace lost or defaced driver's badges"</em>.  Let's hope that such trivial duties don't have to be spelled out in such detail.
