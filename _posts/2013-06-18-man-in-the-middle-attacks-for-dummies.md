Public WiFi security risks are real, not imaginary.  This paper from the Royal Holloway College, University of London highlights <a href="http://lenand.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/wifiworry.pdf">Security Risks</a> associated with the use of public Wi-Fi hotspots.

Should Quarkside publicise this paper?  The advice works both ways.  It gives as many clues to potential criminals as it does to those who should take more care.
